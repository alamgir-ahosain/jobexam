About this work

JobExam started as a way to practice for government and bank job exams without needing a data connection — every question bank, your progress, and your scores are stored locally on your own device.

Each subject ships as its own question bank, so new subjects can be added anytime without disrupting the exams you've already started. Everything works the same whether you're online or completely offline.

  • Feature: 50-question timed exams with instant scoring & negative marking
  • Feature: Personal merit list & exam history, saved per device
  • Offline-first: localStorage-backed progress tracking
  • Focus: Exam-prep & educational tools
