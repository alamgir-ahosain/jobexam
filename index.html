About this work

JobExam started as a way to practice for government and bank job exams without needing a data connection — every question bank, your progress, and your scores are stored locally on your own device.

The whole app is hand-built with plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript — no framework, no build step — so it stays lightweight and works the same offline as it does online. Each subject ships as its own question bank, and new subjects can be added without touching the exam engine itself.

  • Front-end: HTML, CSS, vanilla JavaScript
  • Design: Custom, framework-free UI systems
  • Offline-first: localStorage-backed progress tracking
  • Focus: Exam-prep & educational tools
